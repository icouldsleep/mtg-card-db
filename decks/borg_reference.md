### Logged win — 2026-11-17, four-player pod, won on own turn 9 (log turn 30), 20m32s

sleepy 1 — Desini 0, Peitar 0, Afflon 0. **First game with the deck, straight out of the box.**
Finished on **226 life**, the highest total in any log in this repo. Record 1-0.

Seating was **sleepy** → Desini → Peitar → Afflon, so sleepy's turns were log turns 1, 5, 9, 13,
17, 21, 25, 28 and 30 — **own turn 9**. (Afflon died on log turn 25, Peitar on 28, so the rotation
closes up after that.)

**ONE, BORG EVOLVED IS THE DECK. EVERYTHING ELSE IS A BODY FOR IT.**

{4}{W}{U}{B} 3/3 — *"Artifact creatures you control have flying, double strike, vigilance, menace,
lifelink, and ward {2}."* Six abilities, and in this deck every creature is an artifact creature,
so every creature gets all six.

**Double strike plus lifelink is the engine, not the combat math.** Each attacker connects twice
and every point of it comes back:

| Own turn | Log turn | sleepy's life | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| 7 | 25 | **38 → 100** | **Afflon dead**, 40 → −8. Peitar 29 → 3 |
| 8 | 28 | **97 → 161** | **Peitar dead**, 6 → **−58** |
| 9 | 30 | **161 → 226** | **Desini dead**, 9 → **−56** |

The last two kills were 58 and 56 points past lethal. **Once One, Borg Evolved is down the deck is
not racing anything** — it is deciding when, not whether.

**MOST OF THE WINNING BOARD WAS OTHER PEOPLE'S CARDS.**

This is the thing to understand about the archetype. **Assimilate turns the stolen card into a Borg
artifact creature**, so it instantly picks up One, Borg Evolved's six abilities *and* The Borg
Queen's +2/+2. Six creatures were taken off all three opponents:

| Assimilated | Taken from | What it did |
|---|---|---|
| **Myr Battlesphere** | Desini | Arrived with four Myr tokens; its attack trigger tapped them for **+4/+0 and 4 damage** direct to Peitar |
| **Ethersworn Adjudicator** | Desini | Repeatable *"{1}{W}{B}, {T}: destroy target creature or enchantment"* plus *"{2}{U}: untap"* — **fired twice on log turn 30**, killing Thought Monitor and a Construct token |
| **Etherium Sculptor** | Desini | A 1/2 that attacked for **6** |
| **Wreck Hunter** | Desini | Powerstones off Desini's own freshly-filled graveyard |
| **Heroes in a Half Shell** | Peitar | A 5/5 five-colour legend swinging for sleepy until Peitar spent **Assassin's Trophy** on it |
| **Company Commander** | Afflon | *"Bring it Down! — whenever this creature attacks, creatures you control gain deathtouch until end of turn."* |

**The Company Commander steal was the best of them.** On log turn 25 its attack trigger gave the
whole board deathtouch, and The Borg Queen — blocked by two Zephyrim — killed **both** with 1 and 7
damage in the first-strike step.

**DESINI WAS THE WORST POSSIBLE SEAT TO SIT NEXT TO.** Their deck was Urza, Chief Artificer and
Sharuum the Hegemon artifacts, which means **their whole library is artifact creatures** — exactly
what The Borg Queen's attack trigger digs for. Every Queen trigger in the game targeted Desini, and
**four of the six steals came out of their library.** Their two Steel Overseers spent the game
pumping a board that sleepy kept taking apart with their own Adjudicator.

**RULING — REPLICATE COPIES ARE TOKENS, SO COPYING A LEGEND KILLS IT, AND YOU STILL KEEP THE CAST
TRIGGERS.** Hugh, First of Many gives artifact spells **replicate {2}{U}**. On log turn 25 sleepy
replicated **One, Borg Evolved**; the copy entered and went straight to the graveyard on the legend
rule (`One, Borg Evolved (422) was put into Graveyard from Battlefield`) — **and both Door of
Destinies triggers fired anyway**, because replicate copies are *cast*.

So replicating a legendary artifact creature buys you counters and triggers, not a body. It worked
cleanly on the nonlegendary targets: **Door of Destinies ×2** (log turn 21) and **Swiftfoot Boots
×3** (log turn 28).

**RULING — STATION IS A TWO-TURN SETUP THAT GRANTS THE TEAM EVASION.** Borg Scout Sphere's Station
reads *"tap another creature you control: put charge counters equal to its power on this Spacecraft.
Station only as a sorcery. It's an artifact creature at 6+."* sleepy tapped **Hugh, First of Many
(3 power)** on own turns 3 and 4 — **3 + 3 = 6 counters**, which both turned the Sphere into a 5/5
artifact creature and switched on its 6+ ability: **artifact creatures you control have flying.**

That is how the deck was connecting for four turns before One, Borg Evolved arrived. **Station
rewards tapping your biggest creature, and sorcery-speed only means you plan it a turn ahead.**

**WHAT THE OPPONENTS WERE PLAYING**

- **Desini** — Urza, Chief Artificer / Sharuum the Hegemon artifacts: Steel Overseer ×2 (one copied
  with March of Progress), Thought Monitor, Digsite Engineer, Kayla's Music Box, Liquimetal Torque,
  Bident of Thassa.
- **Peitar** — Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles: Michelangelo, the Heart, Heroes in a Half Shell, Aggro
  Amalgam, Double Jump, Super Combo (replicated), Assassin's Trophy.
- **Afflon** — Warhammer 40,000: Zephyrim (squad, miracle), Inquisitor Greyfax, The Flesh Is Weak,
  Talisman of Hierarchy.

**Afflon's The Flesh Is Weak is worth a note** — *"nonartifact creatures get -1/-1"* killed their
own three Soldier tokens on the turn it landed. It also makes their countered creatures artifacts,
which does nothing good for them across the table from a Borg deck.

**Only two points of damage were ever dealt to sleepy all game** — Michelangelo, the Heart for 2 on
log turn 11. Nothing else connected.

**Forge's header says "Game Outcome: Turn 15"; the log body runs to turn 30.** `ceil(30/2) = 15`.
**Seventh consecutive confirmation** of the halving rule, now across three decks.
