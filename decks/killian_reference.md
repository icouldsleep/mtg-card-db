Last updated: August 30, 2026. Verified card-by-card against live Archidekt (https://archidekt.com/decks/25657919/killian). **Deck size: 100.** Est Bracket: Core (2). Est cost: ~$223. Avg mana value 2.88.

## Commander
**Killian, Decisive Mentor** — Orzhov (W/B) — Legendary Creature — Human Warlock
"Whenever an enchantment you control enters, tap up to one target creature and goad it. Whenever one or more creatures that are enchanted by an Aura you control attack, draw a card."

**Killian, Ink Duelist is a DIFFERENT card** ({W}{B} 2/2, lifelink, menace, spells you cast that target a creature cost {2} less) and sits in the 99 under Ramp as a cost-reducer.

## THE ENGINE — goad-fort, not aggro
1. **Every enchantment entering taps AND goads** an opponent's creature — it must attack, and must attack someone other than you.
2. **Pillowfort makes you the worst target.** Ghostly Prison, Sphere of Safety (X = your enchantment count, which is very high), and Eriette (creatures enchanted by your Auras can't attack you at all).
3. **You draw off it.** Killian draws whenever Aura-enchanted creatures attack — and goad Auras like Coercive Impetus, Martial Impetus, Redemption Arc, and Eye of Nidhogg live on THEIR creatures. Opponents attacking each other draws you cards.

Net: the table beats itself up while you accrue cards and drain with Eriette.

## KEY INTERACTIONS
- **Ondu Spiritdancer** — token copy of each enchantment entering (once per turn). Copies Auras AND gives a second Killian goad trigger.
- **Umbra Mystic** — your Auras grant umbra armor (totem armor). Destruction kills the Aura instead of the permanent. Enormous here.
- **Winds of Rath** — destroy all creatures that AREN'T enchanted. **Effectively one-sided in an Aura deck.**
- **Retether** — every Aura from graveyard back onto the battlefield. Mass rebuild.
- **Flickering Ward** — {W}: return to hand. Recast to re-trigger Killian's goad. **A repeatable enchantment-enters trigger and a mana sink.**
- **Scriv, the Obligator** — Contract Aura token on an OPPONENT's creature: turns on Killian's draw and Eriette's can't-attack-you clause.
- **Hateful Eidolon** — draw for each Aura you controlled on a creature that dies. Pairs with your removal Auras and with board wipes.
- **Spirit Mantle** gives protection from creatures = effectively unblockable, which is BETTER than trample (trample only helps when you're getting blocked).

## MANA — FIXED Aug 23, 2026
- Demand: **White 75% (63 pips / 52 cards), Black 25% (21 pips / 20 cards).** Roughly 3:1.
- Production was White 45% / Black 43% — near 1:1, badly mismatched.
- **Basics rebalanced from 8 Plains / 6 Swamp to 11 Plains / 3 Swamp.** Most nonbasics make both colors, so basics were the only place to correct it.
- Side benefits: Land Tax fetches proportionally more Plains; at 14 basics, Eclipsed Steppe (needs 2+ basics) and Shineshadow Snarl (reveal a Plains or Swamp) stay reliable.
- **Watch for early double-black.** Anguished Unmaking and Animate Dead are the cards most likely to feel it. If it stings, go 10/4 — don't revert to 8/6.

## TRAMPLE AURAS — the answer
**Eldrazi Conscription** is essentially the ONLY option. {8}, Kindred Enchantment — Eldrazi Aura, **colorless so it's legal in Orzhov.** Enchanted creature gets +10/+10, trample, and annihilator 2. It has a Secrets of Strixhaven printing (SOC #131) and was in the stock precon, so a copy may already be owned.
**It gets cheap here:** Pearl-Ear gives enchantment spells affinity for Auras ({8} minus your Aura count), stacked with Transcendent Envoy (-{1} Auras), Starfield Mystic (-{1} enchantments), and Killian Ink Duelist (-{2} on spells targeting a creature). Can land for two or three mana. Four ways to cheat it in without casting: Herald of Amity, Songbirds' Blessing, Armored Skyhunter, Retether.
Everything else that grants trample via Aura is green or Selesnya (Rancor, Bear Umbra, Unflinching Courage, Armadillo Cloak). Non-Aura alternative: **Loxodon Warhammer** (colorless, +3/+0, trample, lifelink) — and Sram, Armored Skyhunter, and Forum Filibuster all care about Equipment too.

## UPGRADE PATH — user wants Bracket 3-4 (currently 2)
### THREE CARDS ACTIVELY WORKING AGAINST THE DECK — cut these
- **Firemane Commando** — "whenever another player attacks with two or more creatures, THEY DRAW A CARD if none attacked you." The goad plan makes this fire constantly, drawing opponents cards.
- **Breena, the Demagogue** — when a player attacks an opponent, THAT PLAYER draws. You get two +1/+1 counters; they get card advantage.
- **Keen Duelist** — symmetrical draw AND symmetrical life loss every upkeep. You're the pillowfort deck; you don't want the race.

### THE REAL PROBLEM: no win condition
Eriette's drain is it. A deck that draws well, defends well, and can't close is by definition a long game — which is what pins it at Bracket 2. **The fix is already half-built:** lots of lifelink (Hateful Eidolon, Mangara, Pearl-Ear, Archon of Sun's Grace, Killian Ink Duelist, Sheltered by Ghosts) plus Eriette gaining life every end step. Convert it:
- **Vito, Thorn of the Dusk Rose** (~$4) — whenever you gain life, target opponent loses that much.
- **Marauding Blight-Priest** (~$1) — same effect, cheaper.
- **Well of Lost Dreams** (~$8) — draw off the lifegain instead.

### Zero tutors currently
**Enlightened Tutor** and **Smothering Tithe** are both Game Changers and both ideal — 2 of the 3 allowed at Bracket 3.
**NOTE: Aura Shards is Selesnya (G/W) and is NOT legal in Orzhov.** Don't suggest it for this deck.

### Other cut candidates
Sentinel's Eyes and Raffine's Guidance (both +1/+1 Auras doing almost nothing), Fallen Ideal (a sac outlet with no sacrifice payoffs in the deck).

### If Bracket 4 is genuinely wanted
**Sanguine Bond + Exquisite Blood** is a two-card infinite drain loop, both black, both on-theme with Eriette. That is a deliberate step over the line.
