## Deck identity
Classic Azorius stax/control. Tax opponents' spells, lock down interaction (counterspells, "can't attack," "can't search," "abilities can't be activated"), draw cards efficiently, close with board wipes or a hard lock. Highest salt score of any tracked deck — built to be genuinely oppressive by design.
