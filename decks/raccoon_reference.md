# Cards Reference — Bello, Bard of the Brambles EDH Deck

**Last updated: September 16, 2026** — re-pulled and verified against new Archidekt link. Source: https://archidekt.com/decks/26465547/racoon_and_friends — **VERIFIED current as of Sept 16, 2026.**

## Commander
**Bello, Bard of the Brambles** — Gruul (R/G) — Legendary Creature — Raccoon Bard — "During your turn, each non-Equipment artifact and non-Aura enchantment you control with mana value 4 or greater is a 4/4 Elemental creature in addition to its other types and has indestructible, haste, and 'Whenever this creature deals combat damage to a player, draw a card.'"

Key mechanical notes:
- Only active during **your** turn. At end of turn the animation ends — animated permanents are NOT creatures on opponents' turns and **cannot block**.
- Requires mana value 4 or greater. MV3 and under never qualify.
- Excludes Equipment and Auras by type, regardless of mana value.
- If the permanent is already a creature, Bello sets its stats to exactly 4/4 during your turn (can shrink a big body).
- Indestructible from Bello does **not** protect against sacrifice or exile.
- **Bello himself is a Raccoon Bard, not an Elemental.** He has no triggered abilities of his own — his text is a static grant.

- **Street Riot** — {4}{R} Enchantment. "As long as it's your turn, creatures you control get **+1/+0** and have trample." **Not +2/+0.** The turn-only window matches Bello's exactly.
- **Sight of the Scalelords** — "At the beginning of combat on your turn, creatures you control with toughness 4 or greater get +2/+2 and gain vigilance." **Vigilance does NOT fix the blocker gap** (user caught this). Animated permanents stop being creatures at end of turn, so they can't block on opponents' turns whether tapped or not — same trap as the Seedborn Muse rejection. **Its actual value is keeping tap abilities live:** Gilded Lotus (MV5), Thran Dynamo (MV4), and Hedron Archive (MV4) can attack as 4/4s and still tap for mana. Sol Ring is MV1, never animated, unaffected.

## Lightning Greaves
{0} Equipment, Equip {0}. Grants haste and shroud. Free protection for legendary creatures (Grothama, Ghalta, Wildsear, Kodama, Vorinclex, Halana and Alena) against targeted removal.
- **Shroud cuts both ways** — a creature wearing Greaves can't be targeted by YOUR OWN spells/abilities either. It can't receive Halana and Alena's counters, can't be enchanted by Bear Umbra, and can't be the target of Mithril Coat's auto-attach trigger. Equip after those effects resolve, or don't equip that particular body.
- MV0, never animated by Bello, no interaction with the artifact/enchantment plan either way.

## Setessan Champion
Creature - Human Warrior. "Constellation — Whenever an enchantment you control enters, put a +1/+1 counter on this creature and draw a card." With 19 enchantments in the deck this is a real repeatable value engine, not redundant with Eidolon of Blossoms (draw-on-enchantment-ETB) since both trigger off the same events — running both just doubles the draws per enchantment that enters.

## Roaming Throne (own the "Doom Variant" Marvel alt-art printing)
{4} Artifact Creature — Golem, 4/4, Ward {2}. "As this creature enters, choose a creature type. This creature is the chosen type in addition to its other types. If a triggered ability of another creature you control of the chosen type triggers, it triggers an additional time."

- **Name Elemental.** Applies to **every** creature of that type, not one — no cap, no once-per-turn.
- Does **not** double its own triggers ("another creature").
- Does **not** copy — it makes the ability trigger again. Targets and modes are chosen separately per instance (matters for Warstorm Surge: split the damage).
- Does **nothing for Bello** (Raccoon Bard) — but Bello has no triggers of his own, so nothing is lost.
- **Only doubles TRIGGERED abilities.** Static effects are unaffected: Fiery Emancipation, Gratuitous Violence, Berserkers' Onslaught, Street Riot, Gruul War Chant, Unnatural Growth's grant — all static, all untouched.
- **What it doubles in this deck:** Bello's granted draw trigger on every animated permanent · Warstorm Surge · Sandwurm Convergence · Primeval Bounty · Sunbird's Invocation · Thickest in the Thicket · Path of Discovery · Eidolon of Blossoms · Dancing from Dark to Dawn · Sight of the Scalelords (+4/+4) · Unnatural Growth (quadruples P/T).
- Dies with Bello: no animation means no Elementals means nothing to double.
- **UNVERIFIED:** whether Caged Sun's mana ability doubles. It's worded as a trigger but triggered mana abilities have odd interactions. Check before relying on it.

## Layers note — applies across this deck
Bello sets base P/T to 4/4 in **layer 7b**. **+1/+1 counters apply in layer 7d, AFTER — so counters STACK on top of the 4/4 and PERSIST when the animation ends.** Next turn Bello animates again and the counters are still there.

Cards that exploit this: Halana and Alena, Thickest in the Thicket (X counters where X = that creature's power — an animated 4/4 becomes an 8/8), Primeval Bounty (3 counters per noncreature spell), Path of Discovery, Dancing from Dark to Dawn.

**March of the World Ooze** (~$6, considered, not in deck) sets base P/T to 6/6 — also a base-setting effect, so the **later timestamp wins.** Play it after Bello and the board is 6/6 instead of 4/4.

## Dancing from Dark to Dawn (IN as of the Farseek swap)
{3}{G}{G} Enchantment, MV5. "Whenever you cast a creature spell, put X +1/+1 counters on target creature you control, where X is that spell's mana value. Landfall — Whenever a land you control enters, create a 2/2 green Bear creature token."
- Qualifies for Bello (MV5, enchantment, not an Aura). During your turn it's a creature, so it can target **itself**.
- Weaver of Harmony copies its triggered abilities — double Bears off one land drop.
- The counter half is the weaker half post-rebuild: creature count dropped to 21 and most of the cheap dorks were cut, so X is often small. The landfall half is the real value with 35 lands plus Three Visits, Tempt with Discovery, Lotus Cobra, The Regalia.

### Cross-deck layers rule that applies here too
+1/+1 counters (layer 7d) apply AFTER base-P/T-setting effects (layer 7b), so counters stack on top of Bello's animated 4/4. But two base-P/T-setting effects don't stack — later timestamp wins (this is why Sephiroth's 7/5 overwrites Aettir/Priwen's 40/40 in the Tifa deck, a related case).
