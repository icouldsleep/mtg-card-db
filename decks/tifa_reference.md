## Commander (1)
**Tifa, Martial Artist** — {1}{R}{G}{W} — Legendary Creature — Human Monk — 4/4
"Melee (whenever this creature attacks, it gets +1/+1 until end of turn for each opponent you attacked this combat.) Whenever one or more creatures you control with power 7 or greater deal combat damage to a player, untap all creatures you control. If it's the first combat phase of your turn, there is an additional combat phase after this phase."

**KEY RULINGS:**
- Melee counts **opponents attacked**, not creatures attacking. Attack all three in a 4-player pod → Tifa is +3/+3 → **7/7, self-enabling her own trigger with no equipment.**
- Her last ability checks **each individual creature's** power, not combined.
- The extra combat **cannot loop** — gated to the first combat phase. Bracket-safe.
- Grants NO additional main phases.

## Creature (2)
**Mirri, Weatherlight Duelist** — First strike. On attack, each opponent can't block with more than one creature. **While TAPPED, no more than one creature can attack you each combat.**
  - The attack trigger is a **PER-OPPONENT cap** — it stops MULTI-BLOCKING, it is not blanket evasion.
  - **ANTI-SYNERGY: Red XIII grants vigilance to modified creatures.** An equipped Mirri with Red XIII out doesn't tap when attacking, which **turns off her defense.**
  - Tapping her after attackers are declared does not remove them from combat.
**Thorin, Mountain-king** — Trample. ETB: attach any number of Equipment to one creature, then that creature deals damage equal to **its power** to a target creature.
  - Attach is FREE (triggered ability, not the equip ability). **Best line: attach Aettir and Priwen** — at 40 life the creature is a 40/40 and deals 40 to a creature.
  - Melee does NOT apply (his trigger is on entry, in your main phase).
  - **TENSION:** pushes everything onto one creature; Cloud Ex-SOLDIER wants gear spread. Ardenn redistributes at combat.

**White is over half your pip demand and gets under a third of production. Red and green are both OVER-supplied relative to need.**

Basics are **3 Plains / 3 Mountain / 3 Forest** — even, when demand is 53/27/20.

# BRACKET — a high 3

**Three Game Changers: Smothering Tithe, Enlightened Tutor, Aura Shards** — exactly the cap. A fourth forces Bracket 4.

**Why it's a legitimate 3:** wins through combat damage, the most interactable win condition. No infinite combo, no tutor-into-kill, no fast mana past Sol Ring. **Why it's not a 2:** five Equipment tutors, Puresteel/Astor/Bruenor/Forge Anew cost reduction, and Nahiri casting Equipment free make it far faster than Core expects.

**Social note:** salt 46, ~$1,263, and it LOOKS like a 4 across the table. Say "Bracket 3, no combo, wins through combat" up front.

**DO NOT ADD:** Godo Bandit Warlord (infinite combats with Helm of the Host), Aggravated Assault (infinite combats with Sword of Feast and Famine), any Armageddon-style mass land denial.

### Layers ruling that applies here
+1/+1 counters (layer 7d) apply AFTER base-P/T-setting effects (layer 7b) — they stack on top. But two base-P/T-setting effects do NOT stack; later timestamp wins. **This is why Sephiroth's 7/5 overwrites Aettir and Priwen's 40/40** if Sephiroth's effect is applied later.
